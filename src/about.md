# About

I'm nacren. Mostly I write about Unicode and the odd things it lets a document hide from the person reading it.

The first piece is [Cursed Strings](/posts/cursed-strings/). I tried to slip hidden instructions past six local models, four different ways. The invisible tricks failed. The readable ones worked, and cleaning the text sometimes made them worse.
